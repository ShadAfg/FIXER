# 🔧 FIXER — Every Skill. One Click. Fixed.

FIXER is a local services marketplace that connects customers with trusted 
skilled workers in their area — electricians, plumbers, carpenters, civil 
engineers, masons, farmers, cleaners, mechanics and more.

Users can **browse categories, search for pros, compare ratings, and book 
services**. Skilled workers can **register and list their services for free**.

## ✨ Features

- 🔍 **Live search** — filter service categories instantly as you type
- 🗂️ **12+ service categories** — Construction, Carpentry, Electrical, Plumbing,
  Agriculture & Farming, Cleaning, Painting, AC Repair, Locksmith, Auto Repair,
  Packers & Movers, General Labor
- ➕ **"Add Your Service"** — providers can register and list themselves
- 📊 **Trust stats** — vetted pros, average rating, response time
- 📱 **Fully responsive** — works on mobile, tablet & desktop
- 🎨 **Teal + Yellow theme** with full-screen hero photography
