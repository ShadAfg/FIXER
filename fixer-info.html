Service information

Helpful details before you choose or book a provider.

Why choose FIXER

Compare provider profiles before you send a request. Listings can show a provider's service category, city, hourly rate, experience, certification information, and customer reviews.

Use the hire form to share your preferred meeting location, date and time, phone number, and job details in one request.

Fair services

Providers set their own prices. Review the listed hourly rate and confirm the total cost, materials, travel, timing, and payment terms with the provider before agreeing to a job.

Certification details are self-reported unless independently verified. Sample profiles and sample reviews are marked as demo content.

City services

Choose a service category and enter your town or city in the service matcher. FIXER filters the available listings by category and matching location text.

City-to-city jobs for providers

For work between cities, include the origin, destination, job scope, and preferred schedule in your request. Ask the provider to confirm service area, travel or transport costs, and availability before you agree.

This is a static demo. Listings are stored in the visitor's browser, and booking requests open an email draft. A request is not a confirmed booking until it is sent and accepted.
